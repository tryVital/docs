<Warning>
The legacy Fitbit integration is retired and no longer synchronizes data. Connect new users through [`google_health`](/wearables/guides/google-health). Existing Fitbit users must [authorize a new Google Health connection](/wearables/guides/google-health#migrate-from-fitbit).
</Warning>

Fitbit devices remain supported through Google Health. The retirement applies to the legacy integration, not the hardware.

## Time Zone resolution

This section describes historical records from the retired `fitbit` integration. It does not describe Google Health timestamp handling.

Fitbit stored most data without time zone information. To convert those records to UTC, Junction used the first available time zone source:

1. The Fitbit Profile, when the user granted the Profile scope.
2. The most recently recorded Fitbit activity, when the user granted the Activity scope.
3. The [user fallback time zone](/wearables/providers/timestamps-and-time-zones#user-fallback-time-zone), when set.
4. UTC (+00:00), when no other time zone was available.

Historical timestamps may be inaccurate when the required scopes or time zone information were unavailable. See [Timestamps and Time Zones](/wearables/providers/timestamps-and-time-zones) for general timestamp conventions.
