For a comprehensive Dashboard-focused overview of turnaround times, check out [the product guide](https://support.junction.com/articles/9137043704-turnaround-time) (its p95/p99 estimates describe the deprecated TAT behavior). This page covers the current percentile estimates and API behavior.

Turnaround time (TAT) measures the time from sample collection to results. Marker responses include historical p50, p90, and p95 estimates over 7-day and 90-day windows.

The existing `common_tat_days` and `worst_case_tat_days` fields use the p90 and p95 estimates over the past 90 days, respectively.

Turnaround time estimates apply at three levels:

1. Markers
2. Panels
3. Orders

<Note>
Turnaround times are calculated from historical data. Estimates are `null` when there is insufficient data to calculate them.
</Note>

<Warning>
We recalculate turnaround time estimates regularly using the latest historical data. Values may change to reflect real-world logistical circumstances.
</Warning>

## Historical percentile estimates

A percentile describes the historical distribution of turnaround times: p50 is the median, p90 is the 90th percentile, and p95 is the 95th percentile. For example, a p90 estimate of 5 days means 90% of the historical turnaround times were 5 days or less. These describe historical results rather than guarantees for a particular order.

| Field | Percentile | Historical window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `p50_tat_days_past_7_days` | 50th | 7-day |
| `p90_tat_days_past_7_days` | 90th | 7-day |
| `p95_tat_days_past_7_days` | 95th | 7-day |
| `p50_tat_days_past_90_days` | 50th | 90-day |
| `p90_tat_days_past_90_days` | 90th | 90-day |
| `p95_tat_days_past_90_days` | 95th | 90-day |

Estimates are expressed in calendar days.

All six fields are included in marker responses and can be `null` when unavailable. They are not returned at the top level of a panel.

## Marker turnaround times

Marker turnaround times are based on historical orders of each marker. Responses containing marker data, such as [retrieving markers for a lab test](/api-reference/lab-testing/lab-test-markers), include the six percentile fields listed above. Each estimate can be `null` when unavailable.

The existing `common_tat_days` and `worst_case_tat_days` fields remain available for backward compatibility and now reflect the p90 and p95 estimates over the past 90 days, respectively.

## Panel turnaround times

Panel turnaround times are based on the largest corresponding estimate among the panel's markers. Responses containing lab test data, such as [retrieving a lab test](/api-reference/lab-testing/get-lab-test), include `common_tat_days` and `worst_case_tat_days`. Each estimate can be `null` when unavailable. If any marker's corresponding estimate is `null`, the panel estimate is also `null`.

The `common_tat_days` and `worst_case_tat_days` fields reflect the p90 and p95 estimates over the past 90 days, respectively, expressed in calendar days.

## Order turnaround times

Order turnaround times are based on the panel's estimates and the sample collection time. Responses containing order data, such as [retrieving an order](/api-reference/lab-testing/get-order), include the panel's turnaround time fields in `lab_test` and two date fields at the top level. Each date can be `null` when the corresponding panel estimate or sample collection time is unavailable.

| Field | Estimate |
| --- | --- |
| `expected_result_by_date` | Collection time plus the panel's `common_tat_days` (p90 over the past 90 days). |
| `worst_case_result_by_date` | Collection time plus the panel's `worst_case_tat_days` (p95 over the past 90 days). |

## Backward compatibility

Existing fields remain available for backward compatibility, but their values now use the newer percentile estimates and may differ from previous values.

| Existing field | Current behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Marker `common_tat_days` | Uses `p90_tat_days_past_90_days`, replacing the previous p95 estimate. |
| Marker `worst_case_tat_days` | Uses `p95_tat_days_past_90_days`, replacing the previous p99 estimate. |
| Panel `common_tat_days` and `worst_case_tat_days` | Use the largest corresponding p90 and p95 estimates over the past 90 days among the panel's markers. |
| Order `expected_result_by_date` and `worst_case_result_by_date` | Use collection time plus the corresponding percentile-based panel estimate. |

Existing estimates can change or become `null` when historical estimates are unavailable. Order date fields are also `null` until sample collection has occurred.

## Order tracking and ETA revisions

Order tracking provides completion estimates and revisions for a specific order. These are separate from historical TAT estimates, which describe how long similar tests have taken.

Use [Get order tracking](/api-reference/lab-testing/get-order-tracking) to retrieve:

- Original and current estimated completion times in `expected_at.final_results`.
- Collection information in `collected_at`, or `null` when the collection time is unavailable.
- Ordered markers and their completion estimates and progress statuses. `markers[].results` lists expected result markers and any additional received result markers. An entry does not mean its result has been received; use `markers[].status` to determine completion.
- ETA revisions in `updates`, including their reason, source, and affected markers.

Marker progress uses these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pending` | No required results have been received for the marker. |
| `incomplete` | Some, but not all, required results have been received. |
| `resulted` | All required results have been received. |

The Junction operations team may update an order's estimated completion time as new information becomes available. Each revision's `source` identifies whether the information came from the lab (`lab`), Junction (`junction`), or the patient (`patient`). New status and source values may be added. Include a default case when handling these fields.

Completion estimates can be `null` when unavailable. `expected_at.final_results.original_estimate` contains the initial estimate, `expected_at.final_results.current_estimate` contains the current estimate, and `updates` contains subsequent revisions.

### Webhook notifications

The [labtest.order_tracking.updated](/event-catalog/labtest.order_tracking.updated) webhook captures an immutable tracking snapshot when an ETA revision is recorded. Its `data` contains `order_id`, `expected_at.final_results`, `collected_at`, `markers`, and `updates`, matching the tracking endpoint's response structure.

This webhook requires order tracking access and does not notify you of every collection or result change. Later changes do not alter an earlier event's snapshot; use the tracking endpoint for current state.

For webhook handling, see [Event Structure](/webhooks/event-structure) and [Retry Policy](/webhooks/retry-policy).
