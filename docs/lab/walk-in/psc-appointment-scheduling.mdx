---
title: "Schedule PSC Appointments"
description: "Find appointment availability and book, retrieve, reschedule, or cancel supported Quest PSC appointments."
---

Junction can schedule walk-in appointments at supported Quest Patient Service Centers. Appointment scheduling is optional; patients can generally complete a walk-in order without booking an appointment.

## Before scheduling

- Create a walk-in order for a supported Quest laboratory.
- Use [Area Info and PSC Info](/lab/overview/locations) to find locations with the `appointment_scheduling_via_junction` capability.
- Wait for the order to reach `requisition_created` unless [scheduling before requisition creation](/lab/walk-in/order-lifecycle#scheduling-appointments-before-a-requisition-has-been-created) is enabled for your Team.

<Note>
  Arizona Quest locations are operated by Sonora Quest and require the [Arizona Scheduling](/lab/walk-in/order-lifecycle#arizona-scheduling) feature.
</Note>

## Scheduling workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find availability">
    Call [PSC Appointment Availability](/api-reference/lab-testing/psc-scheduling/appointment-psc-availability) with up to three PSC `site_codes`, or search by ZIP code and radius.

    Appointment start and end times are returned in UTC. Use each location's `iana_timezone` to display local times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book the appointment">
    Pass the selected slot's `booking_key` to [Book PSC Appointment](/api-reference/lab-testing/psc-scheduling/appointment-psc-booking). Save the appointment `id`, `status`, `event_status`, and `external_id` from the response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor confirmation">
    Subscribe to `labtest.appointment.updated`. The appointment object's `status` reports its current provider state, while `event_status` reports its normalized lifecycle state. See the [appointment lifecycle](/lab/walk-in/order-lifecycle#appointment-lifecycle).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage the appointment">
    Use the appointment endpoints to retrieve, reschedule, or cancel the active appointment. Cancelling the lab order does not automatically cancel a PSC appointment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Appointment endpoints

| Operation | Endpoint |
| --- | --- |
| Find availability | [`POST /v3/order/psc/appointment/availability`](/api-reference/lab-testing/psc-scheduling/appointment-psc-availability) |
| Book | [`POST /v3/order/{order_id}/psc/appointment/book`](/api-reference/lab-testing/psc-scheduling/appointment-psc-booking) |
| Get | [`GET /v3/order/{order_id}/psc/appointment`](/api-reference/lab-testing/psc-scheduling/get-psc-appointment) |
| Reschedule | [`PATCH /v3/order/{order_id}/psc/appointment/reschedule`](/api-reference/lab-testing/psc-scheduling/appointment-psc-rescheduling) |
| Cancel | [`PATCH /v3/order/{order_id}/psc/appointment/cancel`](/api-reference/lab-testing/psc-scheduling/appointment-psc-cancelling) |
| Get cancellation reasons | [`GET /v3/order/psc/appointment/cancellation-reasons`](/api-reference/lab-testing/psc-scheduling/appointment-psc-cancellation-reasons) |

## Provider appointment ID

The `external_id` is the appointment identifier assigned by the laboratory provider. Make it available to the patient because the PSC may use it to locate the appointment. Junction includes it in supported [appointment SMS communications](/lab/workflow/communications#appointment-communications).
