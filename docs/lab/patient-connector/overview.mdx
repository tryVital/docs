The lab testing connector lets you order lab tests from your lab testing provider, book a visit to a patient service center and read your results, all by chatting with your AI assistant.

With the connector, you can:

- **Find the right test**: see the panels your provider offers, what each one measures and its price.
- **Order and pay**: confirm the panel and price, then pay on a secure checkout page.
- **Book your visit**: find appointment times at patient service centers near you, and book, move or cancel your appointment.
- **Track your order**: see where your order stands and what to do next.
- **Read your results**: see each marker and which are out of range, and download the lab's PDF report.

Access is optional and secure, and you can disconnect at any time.

## Getting started

### Before you start

- **An AI assistant that offers the lab testing connector.** Look for it in your assistant's connectors or apps. If your assistant supports custom connectors instead, your provider gives you a connector URL that looks like `https://<provider-id>.mcp.us.junction.com/`.
- **A US address.** Lab testing is available only in the US, at US patient service centers.

### Connect

1. In your AI assistant, open its connectors or apps and choose the lab testing connector. For a custom connector, add a new one and paste your provider's connector URL.
2. Click **Connect** and approve the connection.
3. Return to your assistant. The connector is now active in your conversations.

Once you're connected, start a new conversation and ask "What can you help me with for lab testing?"

## How connecting works

The connector uses OAuth 2.0, so you never share a password with your AI assistant.

1. When you first connect, you approve the connection in one step. You can browse panels and prices straight away.
2. The first time you order, the checkout page asks for your details and your consent, including HIPAA authorization, before you pay.
3. Your assistant can only reach your own lab testing account. If your connection expires, you'll be asked to reconnect.

## What you can do

### Find the right test

Ask about the panels your provider offers. Your assistant shows what each one measures, its price for your state and whether you need to fast beforehand. If another lab is cheaper, quicker or closer for you, your assistant tells you the trade-off. Tax is added at checkout.

**Try asking:**

- "What lab tests can I order?"
- "Which test checks my cholesterol?"
- "How much is the thyroid panel in California?"

### Order and pay

Once you've chosen a panel and agreed to its price, your assistant gives you a link to a secure checkout page powered by Stripe. That page collects the details the lab needs and your consent, then takes payment, so your card details are never shared with your assistant.

**Try asking:**

- "I'd like to order the diabetes panel."
- "Which lab will process my test?"

### Book your visit

Your assistant finds appointment times at patient service centers near your zip code. You can pick a time at least a day ahead before you pay, so it's held for you while you check out. For an earlier time, pay first and then book. You can also walk in with your requisition form.

**Try asking:**

- "What's the earliest appointment near 90210?"
- "Can I move my appointment to next week?"
- "Send me my requisition form."

### Track your order

Your assistant shows where each order stands and what happens next, from your visit to your results. To cancel an order, contact your provider's support; your assistant can give you their details.

**Try asking:**

- "Has my payment gone through?"
- "Where's my order?"
- "When will my results be ready?"

### Read your results

When your results are in, your assistant lists each marker with its value, shows which ones the lab flagged as out of range and shares the lab's PDF report. It doesn't interpret your results or give medical advice, so review them with your clinician.

**Try asking:**

- "Are my results ready?"
- "Which of my results are out of range?"
- "Send me my results PDF."

## Data privacy and security

### What your assistant can access

When you ask, your assistant can see:

- the details on file for you: name, date of birth, sex, phone number, email and address
- your lab orders, appointments and their status
- your lab results and documents

### What your assistant can't access

- your card or payment details, which stay on the Stripe checkout page
- other patients' information
- anything outside your lab testing account with your provider

### Security measures

- OAuth 2.0 with PKCE for sign-in
- HTTPS for all data in transit
- access limited to your own account
- links to documents such as your results PDF expire after 15 minutes

### Your control

- Disconnect the connector from your AI assistant's settings at any time.
- Your assistant asks you to confirm before it pays for, books or cancels anything.
- Ask your assistant to correct your details, or contact your provider's support to delete your information.

## Troubleshooting

### You're asked to reconnect

**Cause:** your connection has expired.

**Fix:** in your AI assistant's connector settings, reconnect the connector.

### Your assistant can't find any tests

**Cause:** your assistant needs your state to show prices.

**Fix:** tell it which state you live in, or share your address.

### No appointment times are available

**Cause:** there are no open slots near the zip code or on the dates searched.

**Fix:** try a nearby zip code or a later date, or walk in with your requisition form.

### Your results aren't showing

**Cause:** the lab hasn't sent them yet.

**Fix:** ask your assistant where your order stands. It tells you when results are expected.

### A document link doesn't open

**Cause:** document links expire after 15 minutes.

**Fix:** ask your assistant for a new link and open it straight away.

## Best practices

- **Be specific.** "Which test checks my cholesterol and how much is it?" gets a better answer than "What should I test?"
- **Check your details at checkout.** Make sure your name, date of birth and address are right before you pay, so your order reaches the lab correctly.
- **Follow up with your clinician.** Your assistant shows your results but doesn't interpret them. Review anything out of range with your clinician.

## Getting help

- **Orders, payments and results:** ask your assistant for your provider's support contact, or contact them directly.
- **Connecting your AI assistant:** see your assistant's help center.

## Technical specifications

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Protocol | [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) (MCP) |
| Transport | Streamable HTTP |
| Authentication | OAuth 2.0 authorization code with PKCE, using Client ID Metadata Documents |
| Protected resource metadata | `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource` (RFC 9728) |
| Scopes | `mcp:read`, `mcp:write` |
| Regions | US |
