With on-site collection, the patient has their sample collected at a customer-managed location rather than a laboratory-owned Patient Service Center. Collection locations can include clinics, employer health centers, and event-based wellness programs.

## How it works

1. Place an order through the Junction Dashboard or the [Create Order endpoint](/api-reference/lab-testing/create-order).
2. Junction validates the order and creates a requisition with the selected partner laboratory.
3. The patient attends the customer-managed collection location at the time defined by the collection program.
4. Collection staff draw the sample and arrange its delivery to the laboratory.
5. The laboratory processes the sample.
6. Junction makes results available as structured data and a PDF. See [Result Formats](/lab/results/result-formats).

## What to plan for

- **Collection logistics:** Your program is responsible for defining where and when collection occurs.
- **Patient instructions:** Tell patients when and where to attend and provide any preparation instructions associated with the order.
- **Availability:** On-site collection must be enabled and configured for your Team before you place orders.

## Preparing an on-site draw in the Dashboard

After creating an on-site order, select **View instructions now** in the success
notification. You can also open the saved order and choose **Start on-site collection**
while its sample ID is being assigned.

1. **Tubes & instructions:** Review tube types, quantities, and the collection and
   processing instructions returned by the laboratory. Guidance is available for
   Quest, Labcorp, and BioReference. Retry a failed
   request or use the order's collection-instructions PDF when available.
2. **Labels:** Check the collection time and number of labels, then generate the
   PDF. The time defaults to your current local time and is printed on every label.
   Preview, open, or print the PDF. Changing the time or count requires regenerating
   the labels before continuing.
3. **Confirm collection:** After the blood has been collected and the tubes labeled,
   select **Yes, mark as collected**. This updates the order to `draw_completed`.
   Choose **Not yet** to leave without recording collection.

Generating or printing labels does not mark the draw complete. API integrations
can record a completed draw with [Update Order Draw Completed](/api-reference/lab-testing/update-order-draw-completed).

Instructions support direct links to a saved order:

```text
/team/{team_id}/{environment}/lab-testing/orders?order_id={order_id}&collection=instructions
```

The link requires normal Dashboard access to that team and order. It opens the
existing order-details drawer and instructions, including after a reload. Closing
the instructions returns to the drawer; closing the drawer leaves the Orders page
and Dashboard navigation available.

Instructions are available while the sample ID is being assigned. An amber
**Awaiting assignment** badge appears beside **Sample**, and the Dashboard checks
for assignment automatically. **Check again** refreshes the saved order manually.
Labels remain unavailable until the sample ID arrives. After a draw is recorded,
the start action is hidden; linked instructions remain available for viewing
without another collection submission. Cancelled and resulted orders have
read-only instructions and retain existing downloads.
